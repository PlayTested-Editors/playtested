    How an article goes live

    You write; the chief editor checks it and publishes.

    1
    Write youStart a new article. Everything saves automatically, and History keeps every version.
    2
    Submit for review youWhen it's ready, press Submit for review. Images are optional; the chief can add them.
    3
    Review chief editorThe chief either publishes it or asks for changes. Notes show at the top of your article, and it appears under "Changes requested".
    ✓
    Live on PlayTested.netWith your byline on it. You can still suggest edits later; they go through review again.
    As an editor you can also open and improve anyone's draft, then submit it for review on their behalf.
